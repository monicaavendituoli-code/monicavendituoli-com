Hello, I'm Monica

I connect people. First through content, now through conversion.

Marketing technology leader · Former journalist · Duke MBA

For go to market teams losing qualified demand in the gap between a click and a conversation, I build both halves of the fix, the content that earns the click and the systems that route it. I own Contact Sales CTAs at Microsoft, a $100M FY26 revenue pipeline and more than 151.6K qualified leads across 70 countries.

I got here by way of a newsroom and a PR agency. As a journalist I connected the public to the politicians and business leaders whose decisions shaped their lives. In public relations I connected those leaders back to reporters. The through line is the same in all three, find the reason two people would want to talk to each other, then remove everything standing between them.

That background is why I write like the reader matters and measure like the pipeline does. And I'd love to connect with you to see what brings you here.
